Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Introduction

Rallio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a rally obedience training app developed by Alexander Ohlsson. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Display name (optional, for personalization)

Training Data

  • Courses you create
  • Training sessions and run times
  • Training statistics and history

Device & Usage Data

  • Anonymous analytics data (app usage patterns, screen views)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Device type and operating system version

Advertising Data

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) — only if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS) or do not opt out of personalized ads (Android)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps)

Purchase Data

  • Subscription status (free or premium)
  • Purchase history is processed by Apple/Google and RevenueCat; we do not store payment details

How We Use Your Data

  • App Functionality: Your account and training data are used to provide the core app experience — saving courses, timing runs, and tracking progress.
  • Analytics: We use PostHog and Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. This data is anonymized.
  • Advertising: Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
  • Error Tracking: We use Sentry to detect and fix crashes and bugs.
  • Subscriptions: We use RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions.

Tracking & Advertising

On iOS, the app asks for your permission on first launch to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). This is used solely for serving personalized ads through Google AdMob.

On Android, personalized ads are served by default but can be opted out of in your device's Settings > Google > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

If you decline tracking (iOS) or opt out (Android), you will still see ads but they will not be personalized. You can change these settings at any time.

Premium subscribers do not see any ads.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, analytics
  • Google AdMob — Advertising for free users
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • PostHog — Product analytics
  • Sentry — Error and crash reporting

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud). Training data is also cached locally on your device for offline access using encrypted storage.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of ad tracking at any time
  • Export your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at apprallio@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Rallio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
