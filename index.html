FAQ

How do I create a course?

Tap the Courses tab, then tap "Create Course" to start building your rally obedience course with the visual course builder.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! Rallio works fully offline. Your data syncs automatically when you reconnect to the internet.

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription will be restored automatically.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and tap "Delete Account". This will permanently remove all your data.
